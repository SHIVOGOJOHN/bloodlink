Data Policy

How BloodLink handles the data needed to coordinate blood donation safely.

Minimum necessary data

BloodLink collects the minimum account, contact, role, location, blood type, request, and inventory data required to match donors, hospitals, and blood banks.

Consent and transparency

Donor data should only be used for coordination, safety checks, notifications, and audit records connected to blood donation workflows.

Protection and retention

We follow data minimization, consent, access control, and retention principles aligned with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.
