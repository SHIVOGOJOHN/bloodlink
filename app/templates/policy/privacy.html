Privacy Policy

How BloodLink protects personal, donor, hospital, and blood bank information.

Information we use

BloodLink may use names, emails, phone numbers, roles, counties, blood types, donation records, request details, stock records, and operational notifications.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate users, coordinate blood requests, match donors, manage inventory, send reset emails, and keep operational records.

Sharing

Information should be shared only with authorized users involved in the donation, request, fulfillment, reimbursement, compliance, or support workflow.

Your choices

You can request correction, access, or deletion where legally and operationally appropriate. Some records may need to be retained for safety and audit purposes.
