Blood bank staff — How to use BloodLink

Overview and best practices for blood bank staff.

Overview

The BloodLink dashboard helps you see nearby hospital requests, manage stock, and respond to urgent needs.

Stock management

Update your available units and expiry dates regularly. Accurate stock information helps hospitals find you faster when they need supplies.

Requests

Incoming hospital requests will appear on the dashboard. Use the contact details to coordinate transfers and record fulfillment.

Forecasting

The forecast panel suggests expected demand for the coming days. Use it to prioritize collection drives and manage reserves.

Practical tips

  • Check expiry dates daily and mark expired units promptly.
  • Keep contact details up to date so hospitals can coordinate transfers quickly.
