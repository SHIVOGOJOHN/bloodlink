Donor - How to use BloodLink

Simple guidance for donors using BloodLink.

Overview

BloodLink connects donors with nearby hospitals and blood banks. You can set your blood type, see donation opportunities, and track your donation history.

My profile

Keep your contact details and blood type up to date. If you change contact information, hospitals will be able to reach you when needed.

Donation opportunities

Open donation requests near you appear on the donor dashboard. If you are available, contact the hospital or blood bank to arrange the donation.

Safety and consent

Only donate if you are healthy and meet local eligibility rules. BloodLink keeps your contact details private and only shares them when you choose to respond to a request.

Practical tips

  • Bring a form of ID and your contact phone to the donation site.
  • Eat and hydrate well before donating.
  • Note the date of your last donation in your profile.

Questions

Use the contact form in the app or speak to your local blood bank for guidance. If you suspect an error with your profile, update it and notify support.
